# Exact-SKU Cutout Plate & Optical Rim-Wrap Environment Skill (`R26..R29` Benchmark)

## 1. Core Philosophy ("Tách Nền Khóa Sản Phẩm Chuẩn Góc ➔ Tạo Môi Trường Thực Tế & Đổ Nắng Ôm Viền")

Whenever a product must remain **100% identical to the real SKU** (exact silhouette, buttons/foliage/grille/pot, exact physical scale) while being placed into diverse, photorealistic rooms without any 3D plastic floor or cut-and-paste look:

1. **NEVER feed a raw room photo with an existing 3D plastic floor/wall** if you want new room geometries — the old floor locks the camera horizon and contaminates the room with CGI lighting.
2. **ALWAYS isolate the product cleanly first (`Cutout Plate Engineering`)** onto a set of **2K `16:9` Plates (`2560x1440`)** at calibrated physical scale (`50%–72%` height, `7.5%–8.0%` bottom floor margin) across multiple horizontal placements (`Left Hero`, `Right Hero`, `Symmetric Pair`, `3/4 Diagonal`), paired with a **`1024x1024` `1.65x` Macro Close-up Reference (`REF_2_CLOSEUP_1024.jpg`)**.
   - *Special case for products with translucent thermal/airflow effects (e.g., `AIRYEAH WHJ-0907` heater)*: Isolate the **entire `[Product + Golden Airflow Ribbon]` composite** cleanly (erasing 100% of the old office walls, windows, and plastic 3D floorboards) so the product and wind stay 100% locked while the entire room and floor surface are generated fresh.
